## ✨ Features

- 📹 **Select any video** and play it as your virtual camera
- 👀 **Live preview** — see your video with its thumbnail banner
- 🔊 **Volume switch** — the person you call hears the video's sound
- 🖥️ **Hard / Soft decoding** for smooth playback
- 💬 **imo call fix** built in
- 🔑 **Key system** — minute, hour & day keys
- 🌙 Dark modern UI

## 📱 Requirements

- Android 7.0 or higher
- ✅ **No root required** — works on **rooted and non-rooted** devices
- Internet connection (key is checked online when the app opens)
